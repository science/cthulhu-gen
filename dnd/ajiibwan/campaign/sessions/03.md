# Session 3 — The Camp Below Kantaroz
*2026-06-21*

**Party:** Ren (Toan), Orkideh (Steph), Lia (Ania), Gereon (Mia). **NPC:** Jiyloo.
**One-off guests:** Timiki (Briar) and Hasochi (Phoenix) — a zany, tonally-mismatched
entrance played for one session only; they provide material support and step back.

## What happened
- Party climbs **Kantaron Summit**, evading a dangerous construct near the wadi and other
  patrolling constructs closer to the summit.
- Finds an **off-trail mountain path** to the Bokkaran tower, separate from the main pass
  trail south/east, and **Jeffrol's camp** near it. Jeffrol and his mercenaries are inside
  the tower for the day; the party sets an ambush at the camp and waits.
- At day's end, Jeffrol and his crew emerge from the tower — **Jeffrol is wearing a
  white-metal bracelet wrought as a long-legged spider,** taken from inside. The ambush
  triggers: the party defeats the mercenaries with the advantage of surprise. Jeffrol runs;
  **Timiki's speed catches him.**
- **The tower itself was entered by Jeffrol's crew, not the party** — treat scenes 2-6 of
  `scenarios/the-tinkerers-strings.md` (the approach, three ways in, tower floors, the
  summoning) as still substantially open for a future delve; see the note there on how far
  a smash-and-grab crew plausibly got in one day.
- On the way down, Jeffrol counter-accuses Orkideh: *"She was arranging to have me killed! I
  had to take the money and the business papers, to run away!"* He reveals the papers are
  held in trust with a merchant **in Tel A'bib** — but refuses to say who, certain that
  naming the holder is a death sentence.
- Session ends **at the gate of Elmingwed's compound in Tel A'bib**, Jeffrol in tow, not yet
  handed over.
